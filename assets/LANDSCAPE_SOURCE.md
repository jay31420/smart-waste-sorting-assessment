# Opening-scene source

The opening scene uses the public-domain painting:

- Granville Redmond, *Road through a field of wildflowers*
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Granville_Redmond_-_Road_through_a_field_of_wildflowers.jpg
- License: Public domain

`tools/build_oil_scene.py` crops the work to 16:9, adjusts its color and canvas
texture, and adds original modern scene elements: cloud accents, an ordinary
two-stream bin, and a faceless person dropping a waste bag.
